Life/ Childhood

When I was young, I spent most of my time training Muay Thai. We would spar everyday from Monday to Saturday.

This is my Muay Thai Club. Muay Thai is a beautiful martial arts, where respect and tendency are most important. I have been doing this for 14 years now, and fought around 500 matches.

If you have a chance to visit Vung Tau (Viet Nam) - it's a beautiful place, you can check out this club. It's a good place to train.
